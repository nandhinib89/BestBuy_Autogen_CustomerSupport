BEST BUY CANADA — DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/returns-and-exchanges/returning-or-exchanging-a-best-buy-product/returning-or-exchanging-a-defective-item

Purpose:
Use for products that are not working correctly or develop a problem after purchase.

Key facts:
- Best Buy provides different options depending on when the product was purchased and, in some cases, the manufacturer.
- The first step is to check the product's applicable return policy.
- Defective products within the applicable standard return window may be eligible for return or exchange subject to policy conditions.
- Outside the standard return window, the customer may need a different service or manufacturer-related path.
- The answer should not assume that every defective product is automatically refundable.
- The agent should ask for relevant details such as purchase timing, product type and whether it was sold by Best Buy or a Marketplace seller when those facts affect eligibility.

Agent use:
Primary: Returns & Support Agent.
Potential collaboration: Product Agent for product/service context.
