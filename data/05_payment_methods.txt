BEST BUY CANADA — PAYMENT METHODS
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/paying-and-purchasing/payment-methods

Purpose:
Use for questions about accepted payment methods.

Key facts:
Online BestBuy.ca orders can be paid using:
- Visa
- Visa Debit
- MasterCard
- American Express
- PayPal
- Best Buy Gift Card
- Best Buy Financing with Fairstone

In-store payment options include:
- Cash
- Debit
- Visa
- MasterCard
- American Express
- Best Buy Gift Card
- Best Buy Financing with Fairstone
- Monthly Subscription

Agent use:
Primary: Payment Agent.
For a payment-status or charged-but-cancelled scenario, combine with the Order Agent.
