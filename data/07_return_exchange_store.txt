BEST BUY CANADA — RETURNING OR EXCHANGING AT A STORE
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/returns-and-exchanges/returning-or-exchanging-a-best-buy-product/returning-or-exchanging-an-item-at-a-store

Purpose:
Use for questions about returning or exchanging an eligible purchase at a Best Buy store.

Key facts:
- Eligible in-store and online purchases can generally be returned/exchanged at Best Buy stores in Canada, subject to exceptions.
- Marketplace purchases cannot be returned or exchanged at a Best Buy store.
- Cell phones bought from a Best Buy Express store have location-specific return rules.
- Customers should verify eligibility first, prepare the product with original packaging and included parts, bring proof of purchase and the original payment method, and visit an eligible store.
- The normal policy has exceptions, including different rules for cellular/wireless products.
