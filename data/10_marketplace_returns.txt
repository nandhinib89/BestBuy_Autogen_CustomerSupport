BEST BUY CANADA — MARKETPLACE RETURNS
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/blta74f084c4c164e6f/blt320a7016406f5e99

Purpose:
Use when the product was sold by a third-party Marketplace seller through BestBuy.ca.

Key facts:
- Marketplace products cannot be returned to a Best Buy store; they are returned to the seller by mail.
- A product page's "Sold and shipped by" information helps identify whether an item is a Marketplace purchase.
- The seller's return policy normally appears on the product page.
- If no seller policy is shown and the item is otherwise eligible, the Help Centre describes a 30-day request period for most unopened/unassembled items, subject to listed exceptions.
- The customer initiates the return through the order and contacts the seller.
- Return shipping is paid by the seller when the product is defective, damaged or incorrect; other reasons can make the customer responsible for shipping.
- Refunds are generally issued to the original payment method after the seller receives the return.

Agent use:
Primary: Returns & Support Agent.
