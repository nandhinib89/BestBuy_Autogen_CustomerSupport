BEST BUY CANADA — RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/returns-and-exchanges/best-buy-return-and-exchange-policies

Purpose:
Use for general eligibility, timing and conditions for Best Buy returns/exchanges.

Key facts:
- Most Best Buy products can be returned or exchanged within 30 days, measured from the in-store purchase date or online delivery date, subject to exceptions.
- Cellular/wireless products purchased with a carrier plan have different rules.
- Products generally need to be in appropriate condition and include required original packaging/items.
- Customers normally need the original receipt/invoice and original payment method.
- Refunds for payment-card purchases go back to the original payment card; gift-receipt returns use store credit.
- Some product categories are non-returnable or have special conditions.
- Large, defective, bundle, bonus and cellular products can have additional rules.
- Marketplace purchases follow a separate policy.
