BEST BUY CANADA — PRODUCT AVAILABILITY AND STORE PICKUP
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/holiday-shopping-at-best-buy/getting-your-gifts-in-time

Purpose:
Use for questions about whether a product is available for shipping or store pickup.

Key facts:
- Product pages show availability information for in-store pickup and shipping, along with an estimated delivery date when available.
- Quick and Easy Store Pickup can be selected when offered for the product/location.
- Best Buy sends an email confirming that a pickup order has been received.
- If the expected pickup confirmation has not arrived, the customer should check their junk/spam folder and allow for processing time.
- Availability is location and product dependent, so the chatbot should not invent stock levels that are not present in its knowledge base.

Agent use:
Primary: Product Agent.
Potential collaboration: Order Agent when the customer has already placed a pickup order.
