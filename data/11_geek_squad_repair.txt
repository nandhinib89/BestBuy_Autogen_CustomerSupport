Purpose:
Use for repair and technical-support questions, especially computers and tablets.

Key facts:
- Geek Squad provides computer repair services for devices regardless of where they were purchased.
- Services include hardware repair, software repair, diagnosis and data recovery.
- A customer can start in-store or online.
- The repair workflow includes an initial diagnosis and quote, followed by repair if the customer proceeds.
- Many repairs are completed within about a week, though timing depends on the repair.
- Customers can receive updates and track repair status.
- Best Buy also has separate Geek Squad services and support options for other product categories.
