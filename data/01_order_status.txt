BEST BUY CANADA — ORDER STATUS
Source: https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/your-order/checking-your-order-status

Purpose:
Use this document for questions about checking an online order and interpreting order-status messages.

Key facts:
- Customers signed into My Best Buy can open Order History, select Online, choose an order, and view its details/status.
- Guest orders can be checked using the order number and the email address used for the purchase.
- If an order has shipped, the customer can use the shipping carrier's tracking service.
- "In Progress" means the order is being worked on.
- "Shipped" means the order has left the warehouse.
- "Cancelled" means the order was cancelled; Best Buy sends a cancellation email within 24 hours.
- "On Hold" means Best Buy needs additional information before processing can continue.
- Cancellation can occur for reasons such as stock issues, pricing/information errors, or quantity limits.
